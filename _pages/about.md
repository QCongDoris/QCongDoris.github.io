I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Mathematics, Statistics and Insurance](https://msi.hsu.edu.hk/en/) at [The Hang Seng University of Hong Kong](https://www.hsu.edu.hk/en/). I received my B.Sc. and Ph.D. in Statistics from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, where I was supervised by [Professor Hoi Ying Wong](https://www.sta.cuhk.edu.hk/peoples/hywong/).

My research interests lie in stochastic analysis, optimal control theory, and their applications in actuarial science and financial mathematics. In particular, my work focuses on continuous-time mortality modelling and dynamic decision-making under delayed information. I am also interested in dynamic portfolio optimization and algorithmic trading, with broader applications to risk management and quantitative finance.

I am committed to research and teaching in financial mathematics, statistics, and actuarial science, and welcomes academic collaboration in related areas.
