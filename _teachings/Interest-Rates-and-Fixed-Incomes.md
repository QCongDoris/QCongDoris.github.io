title: RMSC6001 - Interest Rates and Fixed Incomes
description: Fixed income securities are highly sensitive to the fluctuation of interest rates. Thus interest rate modeling becomes crucial for pricing and managing fixed income securities. This course introduces various types of fixed income securities and interest rate models. It covers the celebrated Heath-Jarrow-Morton (HJM) model as well as some term-structure models including Ho-Lee, Hull-White and the CIR models.
